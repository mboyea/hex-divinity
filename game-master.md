## The region map should indicate the state of the world

The region map generalizes information for the DM across the known world.
This should make it *easy* and *fast* to reflect player actions across the world.
The region map is intended as a tool for the Dungeon Master, though it could be shown to players partially or in full to help communicate options or changes to the game world.

Each hex tile takes half a day to cross, or half that time with a suitable pathway (road, water) and mount (steed, boat).

### Generate map

Prepare a hex map in _non-erasable ink_.
The hex map should be *no bigger than required* to host the first game session.
Write (in _non-erasable ink_) a label in the bottom left of each hex to serve as a coordinate.
Use a centroid coordinate notation, where 0A is the center hex, 1A-1F is the surrounding 6 hex tiles, and 2A-2L is the surrounding 12 hex tiles, and so on.
See @fig:hex-on-square-grid for an example, where it is also shown how to make a hex grid out of a standard square grid.

If you are starting with a new campaign, it is recommended to start in a Small Sandbox (r=3).

## Myth provides gravity to your game world

### Generate myths & omens

Prepare 4 themes; these can be any random word.

> Example: Sunrise String Flowers Sadness

Then, write a literal interpretation of each theme.

> Sunrise: The crux of this magical anaomaly, personified as "The Dawn"
> String: Thin rays of light as an assassin's tool, like garrote wire
> Flowers: A measure of progress into the dawning days; overgrowth from bloom to wilting
> Sadness: "The Dawn" is conflicted against their obligation as an assassin

Finally, derive the myth title as "The" followed by 1 noun.

> "The Dawn"

Next, you may write a short flavor text; a poem or rumor.

> Written in the seams of the morning light lies a deadly weapon, an assassin's plight

Then, combine the themes in pairs of two to create 6 omens, and organize them in chronological order.

> **Sunrise String** In the morning sunlight, thin strands of something seem to coalesce in the shine
> **Flowers String** Flowers bloom in excess; dancing rays of light nip at their petals
> **Flowers Sunrise** Flowers cut down by the light, through wich singing is heard, The Dawn and its plight
> **Flowers Sadness** A dying man is encountered; flours tattered and bloody about his corpse
> **Sunrise Sadness** The Dawn grows desperate under the weight of inevitability; little time remains
> **String Sadness** The Dawn confronts, bringing all the peaking sunlight from the east; unfortunate & inevitable

Lastly, provide a table (one or more) of potential relevant motives behind the myth, and complications.

|#|Motive|Complication|
| ---:|:---:|:---:|
|1|Longs for eternal dusk|Longs for a real challenge|
|2|Answers a malicious prayer|Suffers from mania|
|3|Henchman for a higher power|Longs for beauty|
|4|Arrived for the soul of a friend|Respects the players|
|5|Corrects magical unbalance|Wishes to tend to nature|
|6|Seeks to prevent a bleak prophecy|Seeks a new life|
